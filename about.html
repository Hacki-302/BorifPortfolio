About me

Curious about how technology works.

I’m Borif Gido, a third-year Software Engineering student at Bule Hora University in Ethiopia. I’m interested in building useful digital experiences and understanding the systems behind them.

My background

Learning the craft, one project at a time.

My path into software engineering is driven by curiosity: I like asking how an application is designed, how its data is handled, and what makes it dependable for the people using it. University study gives me a foundation, while small projects help me turn concepts into practice.

I’m especially interested in frontend and backend development, databases, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity. I also enjoy video editing and creative design, which give me another way to think about visual communication and user experience.
